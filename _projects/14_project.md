A machine-learning platform for analyzing market data, combining unsupervised clustering with supervised models for forecasting and segmentation.

**Stack:** Python · Streamlit · K-Means · Random Forest · CatBoost · XGBoost

**Key features:**

- Market segmentation via K-Means clustering
- Predictive modeling with Random Forest, CatBoost, and XGBoost
- Interactive Streamlit interface for exploring results
