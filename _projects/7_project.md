Computational study of error correction performance across the transition from noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) to fault-tolerant quantum computing (FTQC) regimes.

**Stack:** Python · Qiskit · Qiskit Aer · BlueQubit API

**Key contributions:**

- Simulated crossover thresholds between NISQ and FTQC regimes under depolarizing noise models
- Benchmarked surface codes and repetition codes across varying physical error rates
- Identified break-even points where logical error rates drop below physical error rates
