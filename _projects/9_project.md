Published healthcare analytics study using 2018–2024 ambulance dispatch data from Bishkek to identify demand patterns and recommend optimized station placements.

**Stack:** Python · scikit-learn (K-Means, DBSCAN) · Pandas · GeoPandas · Matplotlib

**Key findings:**

- Identified the most common medical problems among women and most vulnerable age groups
- Clustered call locations to reveal high-demand zones and peak activity periods
- Recommended mathematically optimized ambulance station placements based on call density

**Publication:** [elibrary.ru](https://www.elibrary.ru/item.asp?id=82276214) — _Published AAS Ala-Too, May 2025_
