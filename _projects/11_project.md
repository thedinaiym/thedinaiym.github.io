A novel RLHF-style fine-tuning framework that uses fine-grained reward models to guide policy optimization in text-to-image diffusion architectures, improving alignment between generated images and prompt intent.

**Stack:** Python · PyTorch · Diffusion Models · Reward Modeling · RLHF

**Key contributions:**

- Fine-grained, judge-in-the-loop reward signal for policy optimization of diffusion models (beyond scalar preference rewards)
- Improves prompt-image alignment without the instability typical of naive RLHF fine-tuning
- **Accepted for publication at AIST 2026** (Springer Communications in Computer and Information Science)
