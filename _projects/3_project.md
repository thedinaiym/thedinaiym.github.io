End-to-end pipeline addressing the unique linguistic challenges of Kyrgyz for high-fidelity neural text-to-speech synthesis in low-resource settings.

**Stack:** Python · VITS · Matcha-TTS · MMS · rule-based G2P

**Key contributions:**

- Open-source Kyrgyz G2P library handling agglutinative morphology and clitic-triggered stress shifts
- Text normalization covering numbers, symbols, abbreviations, and Latin-script artifacts
- Rule-based stress assignment for interrogative particles and predicative/person suffixes
- Syllabification-driven stress positioning and context-dependent consonant/vowel rules
- Single-speaker dataset creation protocol at 44.1 kHz WAV with studio-grade recording practices
