Automated data collection and interactive visualization of exchange rates and precious metals prices across the Kyrgyz banking sector.

**Stack:** Python · Streamlit · Supabase (PostgreSQL) · BeautifulSoup · Pandas

**Key features:**

- Automated scrapers for 20+ banks and the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (NBKR)
- Persistent storage in Supabase PostgreSQL with scheduled refreshes
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard with historical trends, bank comparisons, and metal prices

**Links:** [Live App](https://all-banks-visual-statistics-kg.streamlit.app/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/thedinaiym/All-Banks-KG-Visual-Statistics)
