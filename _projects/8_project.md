Published research presenting a reproducible pipeline to diagnose structural dependencies in the Kyrgyz Republic's external trade (co-authored with Burul Shambetova).

**Stack:** Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · HS trade data

**Key findings:**

- Structural duality: China-centered import-deficit pole (manufactured goods) vs. gold-driven export-surplus pole (UK / Switzerland)
- HS-to-macro-sector mapping with partner role labels: deficit donor, surplus donor, balanced
- Designed for repeatable monitoring of concentration risk and partner dependencies

**Publication:** [SciProfiles](https://sciprofiles.com/publication/view/83046e2a6589e4ffcade1f782c728578) — _Published 2025_
