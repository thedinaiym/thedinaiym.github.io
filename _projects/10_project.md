MathForge is a thesis project addressing the disparity in access to quality STEM tutoring in Central Asia. Typical LLMs frequently produce mathematical errors that make them unsuitable for rigorous coursework. MathForge solves this by merging the computational accuracy of SymPy with the conversational fluency of Llama-3.

**Stack:** Python · Llama-3 · SymPy · LaTeX · Trilingual NLP (English / Russian / Kyrgyz)

**Key features:**

- Hybrid reasoning: SymPy handles symbolic computation, Llama-3 handles natural language — ensuring mathematically correct answers
- Trilingual 3D avatar tutor speaking English, Russian, and Kyrgyz
- Automated LaTeX generator for educators to reduce content-creation burnout
- Covers higher-mathematics topics relevant to engineering curricula
