    Aesthetics-Aware Generative Makeup Transfer
    Dinaiym Dubanaeva
    Under Review, 2025
    Under Review
    Abs Bib HTML

    We present an aesthetics-aware neural makeup transfer system that overcomes the "blind style transfer" limitation of methods such as BeautyGAN. Our pipeline combines a rule-based expert system — encoding professional makeup artistry knowledge (face-shape analysis, skin undertone classification) — with a conditional GAN based on a Mask-Conditioned U-Net with Adaptive Instance Normalisation (AdaIN). A key contribution is the programmatic generation of paired training data via Poisson Image Editing, eliminating the need for scarce annotated makeup pairs. Our best model achieves SSIM = 0.906, LPIPS = 0.034 at 0.88 ms inference on a single RTX 4080 SUPER GPU — outperforming BeautyGAN on all metrics while being 14× faster.

  2. NLP
    Morphology-Aware Text Normalization and Kyrgyz G2P for Low-Resource Speaker-Adaptive Text-to-Speech
    Dinaiym Dubanaeva
    Under Review, 2025
    Under Review
    Abs Bib HTML

    Kyrgyz TTS remains challenging due to agglutinative morphology, stress shifts triggered by clitics/suffixes, and scarce high-quality paired data. This paper presents a morphology-aware preprocessing pipeline and an open-source Kyrgyz grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) library designed for high-fidelity speaker-adaptive TTS. The pipeline performs text normalization, rule-based stress assignment with explicit handling of interrogative particles and predicative/person suffixes, syllabification-driven stress positioning, and Kyrgyz phonological post-processing rules for context-dependent consonants and vowel length.

  3. DS
    Structural Duality in Emerging Markets: HS-to-Sector Mapping and Partner-Role Diagnostics for the Kyrgyz Republic’s Trade (2019–2024)
    Dinaiym Dubanaeva, and Burul Shambetova
    SciProfiles, 2025
    Abs Bib HTML

    We propose a reproducible data-science workflow to diagnose partner–sector dependencies in the Kyrgyz Republic’s goods trade (2019–2024). HS-based flows are mapped into macro-sectors and transformed into partner indicators (turnover, net trade, import coverage, and role labels). Visual diagnostics and tables reveal a structural duality: a China-centered import-deficit pole in manufactured goods and a narrow gold-driven export-surplus pole concentrated in the United Kingdom and Switzerland.
