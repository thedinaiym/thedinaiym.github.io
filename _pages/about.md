I am an M.Sc. I also build and ship full products end-to-end: AlaToo-Doc (an AI university document pipeline), an ML market-analyzing platform, **MathForge** (an educational platform for higher mathematics), **CampusNest** (a student housing & roommate-matching PWA startup), and a Web3 fractional real-estate DApp that won a prize at **ETH Bishkek 2025**.
